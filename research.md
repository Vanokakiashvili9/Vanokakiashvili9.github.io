# Research

My mathematical interests lie primarily in number theory and related areas of pure mathematics.

## Research Projects

### Islands in Isogeny Graphs with Level Structure

I have worked on a research manuscript concerning isogeny graphs of supersingular elliptic curves equipped with level structure.

The project studies how the presence of level structure affects the properties of isogeny graphs, building on ideas involving endomorphisms and the relationship between elliptic curves and quaternionic orders.

The work extends questions about small non-integer endomorphisms and distances between islands of isogeny graphs from maximal orders to Eichler orders.

**Status:** Completed manuscript; not yet published.

### Elliptic Curves, Isogeny Graphs and Iwasawa Theory

In summer 2026, I participated in a research internship at the University of Ottawa under the supervision of Professor Antonio Lei.

The project explored topics involving elliptic curves, isogeny graphs, and Iwasawa theory. It provided an opportunity to study research questions in number theory and develop my understanding of the relevant mathematical literature.

## Research Interests

My primary interest is number theory and algebra. I am particularly interested in elliptic curves and isogeny graphs, while remaining open to other areas of pure mathematics.

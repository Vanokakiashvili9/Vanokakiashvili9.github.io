## Tutorial Sheets

I prepare mathematical tutorial sheets containing problems of varying difficulty, designed to help students practise fundamental techniques and develop deeper problem-solving skills.

### Number Theory

Tutorial sheets covering topics such as divisibility, greatest common divisors, modular arithmetic, the Chinese remainder theorem, Fermat's little theorem, Wilson's theorem, multiplicative orders, and primitive roots.

### Algebra

Tutorial sheets covering topics such as subgroups, normal subgroups, and centers of groups.

### Analysis I

Tutorial sheets accompanying the Analysis I course.

### Matrix Algebra and Advanced Calculus 2

Tutorial sheets accompanying the Matrix Algebra and Advanced Calculus 2 course.
